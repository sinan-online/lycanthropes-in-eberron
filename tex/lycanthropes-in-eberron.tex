\chapter*{Lycanthropes in Eberron}

What if werewolves of Eberron were twelve different ``tribes'', each impacted by a different moon of Eberron?\par

This book is inspired by \href{https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XxNb80pKTArS2SecLkFQE3nnbXTwt2j9/view}{the homebrewed lunar calendar of Eberron by abexy} and the classical World of Darkness game, Werewolf: the Apocalypse by White Wolf.\par

\section*{Zarantyr, the Storm Moon}
% Kythri, the Churning Chaos - Closest moon to Eberron, greatest effect on tides. Cycles and dates based on nothing.
% WRITING PROMPT: Storm Lords - Authority, wrath, rulership; Get of Fenris - Strength, honor, survival of the strong

\section*{Olarune, The Sentinel}
% Lamannia, the Twilight Forest - Follows the seasons, full every summer solstice and new every winter solstice.
% WRITING PROMPT: Hunters in Darkness - Territory, purity, the hunt, boundary enforcers, sacred places

\section*{Therendor, The Healer's Moon}
% Syrania, the Azure Sky - Brother moon of Barrakas. Full on 9th Rhaan for Boldrei's Feast when Syrania is coterminous.
% WRITING PROMPT: Children of Gaia - Peace, balance, mercy, mediators and healers

\section*{Eyre, the Anvil}
% Fernia, the Sea of Fire - Full for The Ascension on 1st Sypheros. Silver moon with affinity to Silver Flame.
% WRITING PROMPT: Iron Masters - Adaptation, tools, change, technology, flexibility, evolution; Glass Walkers - Modernity, progress, cyberculture

\section*{Dravago, the Herding Moon}
% Risia, the Plain of Ice - Keeps distance from other moons. Embodies stagnation and preservation with same cycle every month.
% WRITING PROMPT: Bone Gnawers - Urban survivors, poverty, adaptability, resilience

\section*{Nymm, the Crown}
% Daanvi, the Perfect Order - First/last quarter for Aureon's Crown on 26th Dravago. Smallest moon that can perform total solar eclipse.
% WRITING PROMPT: Silver Fangs - Aristocratic rulers, kingship, legacy, madness

\section*{Lharvion, the Eye}
% Xoriat, the Realm of Madness - Full for Wildnight on 18th Sypheros when The Fury reaches peak power. Retrograde orbit.
% WRITING PROMPT: Blood Talons - Honor through violence, sacred violence, dominance

\section*{Barrakas, the Lantern}
% Irian, the Eternal Dawn - Sister moon of Therendor. Annual eclipse of Sypheros on 15th Therendor during Sun's Blessing for Dol Arrah.
% WRITING PROMPT: Silent Striders - Wanderers and messengers, exile, secrets, death

\section*{Rhaan, the Book}
% Thelanis, the Faerie Court - Tells the story of the year. Full for every solstice, equinox, and cross-quarter days.
% WRITING PROMPT: Fianna - Celtic warriors and mystics, passion, song, heritage

\section*{Sypheros, the Shadow}
% Mabar, the Endless Night - New phase (not full) when Mabar is close. Most intense during Long Shadows when The Shadow reigns.
% WRITING PROMPT: Bone Shadows - Occultists and shamans, spirit politics, metaphysics, shadowed truths; Shadow Lords - Political manipulators, power, control

\section*{Aryth, the Gateway}
% Dolurrh, the Realm of the Dead - Similar effect on manifest zones as Zarantyr has on tides. Single conjunction with most moons.
% WRITING PROMPT: Uktena - Keepers of secrets and taboos, mysticism, the hidden, forbidden lore; Stargazers - Philosophical mystics, enlightenment, detachment

\section*{Vult, the Warding Moon}
% Shavarath, the Battleground - Farthest moon from Eberron, holds back forces from the stars. Full on 12th Nymm for Brightblade celebrating Dol Dorn.
% WRITING PROMPT: Red Talons - Wolf-first extremists, anti-humanity, wilderness purity

\section*{Crya, the Lost Moon}
% Dal'Quor, the Region of Dreams - The thirteenth moon, destroyed or lost. Associated with the Mark of Death. Occurs between Vult and Zarantyr in the ancient Qabalrin Wheel calendar.
% WRITING PROMPT: Bunyip (extinct) - Dreamtime spirits, spiritual collapse
