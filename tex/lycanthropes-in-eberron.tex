\chapter*{Lycanthropes in Eberron}

What if werewolves of Eberron were twelve different ``tribes'', each impacted by a different moon of Eberron?\par

This book is inspired by \href{https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XxNb80pKTArS2SecLkFQE3nnbXTwt2j9/view}{the homebrewed lunar calendar of Eberron by abexy} and the classical World of Darkness game, Werewolf: the Apocalypse by White Wolf.\par
